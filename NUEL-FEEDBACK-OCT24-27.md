## Build plan

Each phase is committed on its own.

1. **Brand foundation**: palette tokens for light and dark (blue → purple gradient, violet, teal, the secondary colours), neon glow, the VJ mark as SVG and a mark + wordmark lockup, a new coloured icon-tile style, motion basics. Redesigned sign-in and sign-up ("Step into VOIDAJ", logo background, animated logo reveal). VOIDAJ replaces "Interact Space" everywhere.
2. **App shell**: mobile bottom nav Home · Chat · (+) · Community · Profile, and a matching sidebar. A clean full-height divider beside the Ads rail. A Local / Universal switch on the Ads rail.
3. **Home and posting**: For You / Following feed, status (story) uploads, and posts that can be Public or City as well as to a community. Add Hub becomes a list of coloured rows, and Post opens the composer straight away.
4. **Profile**: Followers, Following and Connections as separate counts and lists. About edits in place. More detail, the VOIDAJ ID inside the profile, and Posts / Saved / Badges / Activity tabs.
5. **Communities hub**: For You · My Communities · Discover, a hero card, Popular with photos, Suggested.
6. **Explore, My City and Events**: Explore gets Trending / Suggested / Popular. The map gets chips, and tapping a place switches the suggestions and ads to it. Businesses shows "Coming soon". A new Events page with Upcoming / Trending / My Events.
7. **Quick Jobs**: a search bar, a photo list with prices and timing, category tiles with photos, and "Make an offer".
8. **Chat and notifications**: filter chips, a record of where each conversation started, and a context card at the top of the chat. Notifications get source labels and section tabs.
9. **Business profile preview and Settings**: Settings laid out like the mockup, with Payments shown as "coming soon".
